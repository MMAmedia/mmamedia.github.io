Biography

Biography

Ilia "El Matador" Topuria is a Georgian-Spanish professional mixed martial artist who competes in the UFC lightweight division.

Born on 21 January 1997 in Halle, Germany, Topuria began training in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu at a young age. He later developed into one of the most dangerous all-round fighters in mixed martial arts.

Topuria became the UFC featherweight champion after knocking out Alexander Volkanovski at UFC 298 in February 2024. He defended the title against Max Holloway at UFC 308 before moving up to lightweight.

At UFC 317 in June 2025, he defeated Charles Oliveira by first-round knockout to become UFC lightweight champion. He subsequently lost the lightweight title to Justin Gaethje at UFC Freedom 250 in June 2026.

Known for his powerful boxing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and confidence inside the Octagon, Topuria has established himself as one of the leading fighters of his generation.

Biography

Jon "Bones" Jones is an American professional mixed martial artist widely regarded as one of the greatest fighters in the history of the sport.

Born on 19 July 1987 in Rochester, New York, Jones made his UFC debut in August 2008. He quickly became known for his unusual striking range, creative techniques, wrestling ability and high-level fight intelligence.

Jones first became UFC light heavyweight champion in March 2011 after defeating Mauricio "Shogun" Rua. During his first championship reign, he became known for a long series of successful title defences. He later regained the light heavyweight championship and also won the interim title in 2016.

In March 2023, Jones moved up to heavyweight and submitted Ciryl Gane in the first round to win the vacant UFC heavyweight championship. He defended the title against Stipe Miocic at UFC 309 in November 2024, winning by third-round TKO.

Jones announced his retirement in June 2025 and vacated the heavyweight championship. He later expressed interest in returning, but has not competed since UFC 309. His future in professional MMA remains uncertain.

His professional record includes one disqualification loss to Matt Hamill and one no contest against Daniel Cormier, after their UFC 214 result was overturned.
