Biography

Kye "Turbo" Trimble is an England-based flyweight fighter profile. He has trained alongside his uncle at Team Renegade in Solihull, Birmingham, for seven years.

His approach combines Muay Thai with mixed martial arts, with a greater focus on MMA.

Outside the gym, he puts the gloves down to focus on his college studies in Tamworth. His ambition is simple: to be the best.
