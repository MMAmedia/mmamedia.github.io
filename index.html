Biography

Kye "Turbo" Trimble is a British mixed martial artist competing in the flyweight division. Standing at 5 feet 5 inches and weighing 125 pounds, Trimble has developed a versatile fighting style combining technical boxing, aggressive combinations and submission grappling.

Representing Birmingham, England, he competes under the WOWfc banner. His professional record currently stands at an undefeated 3-0.
